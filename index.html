05 — LITTLE QUESTIONS

Q&A

Everything you want to know
before the next crunch.

Are the chips made from real potatoes?+

Yes. Lay’s potato chips start with real potatoes. Follow the official product links for the ingredients in each flavor.

Which flavor should I try first?+

Start with Classic for a golden crunch, or explore Sour Cream & Onion, Barbecue, Salt & Vinegar, Flamin’ Hot, Cheddar & Sour Cream, All Dressed, Sweet Southern Heat Barbecue, and Bacon Grilled Cheese in the lineup.

How do I keep an open bag crunchy?+

Squeeze out the air, roll the top down, clip it, and store it somewhere cool and dry. Always follow the storage guidance on your pack.

Where can I find allergen information?+

Check the label on your bag and the official product pages ↗. Ingredients and allergens vary by product and market.
